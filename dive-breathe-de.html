Core Capabilities & Diver Benefits

  • Offline Digital Scuba Logbook: Record depth, bottom time, gas mix, tank pressure, water temperature, visibility, and buddy verification 100% offline.
  • Enriched Air Nitrox (EANx) Calculators: Compute Maximum Operating Depth (MOD), Equivalent Air Depth (EAD), Best Mix, and partial pressure of oxygen (ppO2 from 1.2 to 1.6 ATA).
  • Breath-Control & SAC Rate Conditioning: Guided respiratory pacing and CO2 apnea tables to calm underwater breathing, improve buoyancy trim, and lower air consumption.
  • PADI IDC & Instructor Ready: Created by Otilio J. Nunez (PADI Master Scuba Diver Trainer #382245) for student divers, recreational divers, freedivers, and dive pros.
