**Le Ciel de Bophut**

Imagine waking up to breathtaking sunrises and spending your days lounging by the infinity pool, overlooking the turquoise waters of the Gulf of Thailand. At Le Ciel de Bophut, this serene paradise is yours to own. This exceptional boutique resort boasts 12 luxurious villas, each a masterpiece of design and comfort.

Each villa is a tranquil oasis, complete with a private pool, outdoor shower, and sleek, modern amenities. Unwind with a cocktail by the pool or indulge in a rejuvenating spa treatment at the on-site wellness center. As the sun sets, gather with friends and family around the fire pit and enjoy the warm evening breeze.

**Key Features:**

• 12 luxurious villas with private pools and outdoor showers
• Infinity pool and wellness center for ultimate relaxation
• Fire pit and outdoor seating areas for social gatherings
• Breathtaking views of the Gulf of Thailand and surrounding landscape
• Prime location in the heart of Bophut, with easy access to shopping, dining, and activities
